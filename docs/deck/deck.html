The problem

A wallet can be verified and still be unusable.

Every regulated Creditcoin application must answer two questions: who checked this wallet, and is that check sufficient for this asset now?

Per-app KYC
Rebuild everything
Vendor contract, PII flow and evidence operations repeat for every application.
Reusable boolean
“KYC passed”
It hides method, jurisdiction, issuer, freshness and sandbox provenance.

Trust the relayer
A signing key becomes the compliance truth. The trust problem only moves.

Proofmark02
The product

Verify. Prove. Enforce.

Proofmark normalizes what an issuer actually checked and makes it enforceable by any Creditcoin application.

1 · Verify
External credential
Reference KR adapter today. Established providers and regulated issuers become adapters—not competitors we must replace first.
method bits regime
2 · Prove
Attestcoin
The issuer emits on Ethereum. BlockProver verifies the exact source transaction on Creditcoin. No Proofmark oracle signature.
source proof replay-safe
3 · Enforce
Frozen policy
Methods, assurance, age, regime, jurisdiction, issuer and roster freshness determine the answer. Asset contracts bind only to frozen policy.
isVerified RWA gate
Positioning: the Creditcoin-native normalization and policy layer above credential providers.

Proofmark08
Why Creditcoin

Creditcoin becomes the compliance decision layer for credentials issued elsewhere.

More useful reads
RWA, lending and stablecoin contracts query durable eligibility state. Reads remain permissionless and free.
Issuer distribution
One Creditcoin adapter exposes established credentials to multiple applications and policies.
Attestcoin necessity
Proven source events replace the operator signature that otherwise owns cross-chain compliance.
We do not claim current ATC demand from reads: Attestcoin states reads are free. Future spoke-chain writes are roadmap, not revenue today.
